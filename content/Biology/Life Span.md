Life span is not necessarily co-related to their sizes. Whatever the life span death is certain for every organism. except single-celled organisms. 

### Life Span occurs in four stages
1. **Juvenility** -> This is the stage where the organism devlops the capacity to reproduce. 
2. **Maturity** -> Reproduction begins at this stage. 
3. **Ageing** and **senescence** -> Ageing is the progressive detoriation of the body of the organism. Terminal irreversible stage of ageing is called senescence. 
4. **Death** -> Senescence finally leads to death.
