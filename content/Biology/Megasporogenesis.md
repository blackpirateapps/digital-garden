1. The process of formation of megaspores from the **Megaspore Mother Cell** is called [[Megasporogenesis]]. 
2. Ovules generally differentiate single megaspore mother cell (MMC) in the micropylar region of the nucellus. 
3. It is a large cell containing dense cytoplasm and prominent necleus. 
4. The MMC undergoes **Meosis** to form megaspores. It results in **Four megaspores**.
