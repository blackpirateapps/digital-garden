# Characteristics of a sexual reproduction
1. There would be gamet formation. 
2. gametes fuse together to form zygote which then becomes new individual. 
3. Fertilisation would occur. 
4. Mostly found in higher and complex plants and animals. 
5. It is a very complex process. 
6. Offspring is not identical to the parent but rather it is smillar. 

## Phases in life cycle
* From birth to their reproductive maturity is called **Juvenile phase**. 
* In plants from seed germination to initiation of flowering is called **vegetative phase**. 
* The time when organism reproduce offspring is called reproductive phase. 
* Sexually there are two types of flowering -
	* **Monocarpic plants** - These produce flowers only once in their lifetime. After that they die. All **annual**(wheat, rice) and **biennial**(carrot, radish) plants are monocarpic. 
	* **Polycarpic -** They are usually perennial and flowers repeatedly at inervals every year. (apple, mango, orange, grapevine)
* Bamboo species(*Bambusa tulda*) [[flower]] only once in their lifetime, **usually after 50-100 years**. 
* ***Strobilanthus kunthiana*(Neelakuranji) flowers only once in 12 years.** It was last seen on 2018
* On the basis of **time of breeding** there are two types: 
	* **Seasonal Breeders**: They reproduce only at a certain period of the year. (frogs, lizards, most birds, deer etc)
	* **Continious breeder**: They reproduce throughout the year. (honeybee queen, poultry, rabbit, mice, cattle etc)
