For flowering plants [[Pollen Grains]] and Embryo sac, both are **Non Motile**. 
Transfer of pollen grains form the shed of the anther to the stigma is called [[Pollination]]. 

* Flowering plants have made many adaptations for pollination. 
* They make use of external agents to do pollination. 

### Kinds of pollination
Depending on the source of pollen, pollination can be divided into **three types**. 

#### 1. Autogamy
1. when pollen grains from the same flower gets transferred to the stigma, it is called autogamy. ==Autogamy happens between same flower only==. 
2. For flowers which have open anther and open stigma, complete autogamy is rare. 
3. For those cases autogamy requires synchrony in pollen release and stigma receptivity, and also the anther and the stigma should lie close to each other so self-pollination can occur, 
4. For some plants like **Viola (common pansy), Oxalis and Commelina** produce two types of flowers. 
	1. Chasmogamous: Flowers which are similar to flowers of other species, with open exposed anthers and stigma. 
	2. **Cleistogamous**: Flowers do not open at all. Anthers lie close to the stigma, when pollen grains dehisce in the flower buds, pollen grains come in contact with the stigma to effect pollination. this is why they are invariably autogamous because there is no chance for cross pollination. **Cleistogamous flowers produce assured seed sets even in absense of pollinators.** 

#### 2. Geitonogamy
1. Transfer of pollen grains from the anther to the stigma of the **same plant**. 
2. It happens in **same plant**. 
3. it is  functionally cross pollination involving a pollinating agent, but genetically it is self pollination, since pollen grains come form same plant. 

#### 3. Xenogamy
1. Transfer of pollen grains from anther of to the stigma of a **different plant** is called xenogamy. 
2. This is cross pollination and brings genetically different types of pollen grains.
