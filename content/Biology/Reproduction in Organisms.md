Each and every orgnism lives for a certain period of time, the period from birth to death is called [[Life Span]]. 
### Reproduction 
1. Reproduction is a biological process in which an organism gives rise to young ones similar to itself. The offspring grows, mature and then produces more offspring. Thus the cycle continues. 
2. Each organism has evolved its own mechanism. Its habitat, physiology, and several other factors are responsible for how it reproduces. 
3. The reproduction methods are broadly catagorised into two parts. [[Asexual Reproduction]] and [[Sexual Reproduction]].
