## Digestive Gland Secretion

### Salivary Secretion
- 25% of parotids
- 70% submaxially 
- 5% sublingual

### Oxyntic glands
- Neck mucus cell - mucus
- oxyntic cells - HCL and intrinsic factor
- Peptic or chief cells - pepsinogen - precursor of pepsin, gastric lipase

### Pyloric glands
- G cells - gastrin 
- D cells - somatostatin

### Pancreatic secretion
- Proteases - Trypsinogen, Chymotrypsinogen, Proelastase, procarboxy peptidases
- Amylolytic: carbohydrate  splitting - alpha amylase
- Lipolytic- Pancreatic lipase, colipase, cholesterol esterase and phospholipase A2
- DNAase
- RNAase

### Secretion of Bile
- bile acids
- bile pigments
- alkaline phosphatase
- some drugs - doxycycline

### Secretions of intestine
- Lactase
- sucrase
- alpha dextrinase / isomaltase
- maltase

### GI hormones

- Gastrin
- CCK
- Secretin
- GIP - gastric inhibitory polypeptide
- VIP
- Somatostatin
- Histamin
