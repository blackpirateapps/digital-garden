the stIt represents the female reprouctive part of the [[Flower]]. 
1. It consists of pistil. 
2. If there is a single pistil then it is called ==Monocarpellary==. 
3. If there is more than one pistil then it is called ==Multicarpellary==. 
4. In cases of Multicarpellary it can be ==Syncarpous(when multiple pistils are fused together)== or ==Apocarpous (when pistils are free and not fused together)==.
5. Each Pistil has three part, **Stigma, Style and [[Ovary]].** 
6. Sigma serves as a landing platform for the [[Pollen Grains]]. 
7. The **style** is the elongated slender part beneath the Stigma.
