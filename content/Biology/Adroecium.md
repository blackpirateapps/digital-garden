Androecium consists of a whorl of stamens. 

## Stamen
It consists of two parts. 
1. **Filament -> Long and slender stalk of the stamen**
2. **Anther -> Terminal generally bilobed structure.**

The proximal end of the filament is attached to the **thalamas or the petal** of the [[flower]]. 
Number of stamens can differ from [[flower]] to [[flower]]. 

## Anther
1. A typical angiosperm anther is ==**bilobed and dithecous**==
2. Often a longitudinal groove runs lengthwise sperating the theca. 
3. It is a tetragonal (four sided) structure. 
4. Each corner has a **microsporangia**. So there is four microsporangia.
5. Microsporangia devlops further and becomes pollen sac. 
6. they extend longitudinally all through the length of the anther.
7. They are packed with **[[Pollen Grains]]**.

### Structure of Microsporangium
1. It is near cicular in outline. 
2. It is srrounded by four wall layers. 
	1. epidermis -> this is the outermost layer and provides protection and helps in dehiscence 
	2. endothecium -> it's function is same as epidermis
	3. middle layers -> same function as epidermis
	4. tapetum - nourishes the devloping [[pollen grains]]. It has dense cytoplasm and generally **more than one neucleous**. 

> Tapetum is of two types: 
> 	1.  Amoeboid tapetum / invasive tapetum / periplasmodial tapetum : Found in primitive angiosperms. It provides neutrition to the devloping microspores after degeration. **E.g: *Alisma* ,*Typha***
> 	2. Glandular or secretory tapetum: Usually found in most flowering plants. It does not degerate. It absorbs neutrients form the middle layer and secretes into the pollen sacs.

When the anther is young compactly arranged homogenous cells occupies the center of each microsporangium. 

MIcrosporangium takes part in [[Microsporogenesis]]. Which leads to the formation of [[Pollen Grains]]
