
## Bee Diseases
- **Bacterial Diseases**
	- American Foulbrood Disease (AFD) :  *Paenibascillus larvae*
	- European Foulbrood Disease (EFD): *Melissococcus pluton*
- **Fungal Diseases** 
	- Chalkbrood Disease : *Ascosphaera apis*
		- Observed in larvae that is three to four days old. 
		- Prevention: Administration of sucrose syrup (1:1) acidified with lemon juice, vinegar or ascorbic acid powder until ph4. 
	- Stonebrood disease : *Aspergillus fumigatus*, *A. niger*, 
		- Mummification of the brood
		- The outer covering of the dead larve becomes hard and black hence the name stonebrood. 
		- No treatments to eradicate. 
- **Viral diseases**
	- SacBrood Disease - Sacbrood virus, *Morator aetatulas* virus
- **Protozoan disease**
	- Nosema disease - *Nosema apis*
		- Worker bees are unable to fly.
		- Worker bees got diahhrea. 

### Honey Bee mites
- Acrosis (Honey Bee Tracheal mite) , acarine disease. - *Acarapis woodi*
	- lives in the trachea of the bees, feed on hemolymph.
	- effects worker, drones and queen
	- **Buckfast bee** : bees ressistant to acrosis. 
- Varroasis - *Varroa destructor*
	- Can be seen with naked eye  as small red or brown spot on bee's thorax. 
	- they reproduce on pupae and feeds on hemolymph. 

**Apivar**; It is a polymer strip used to kill varroa mites
**Apistan**: Miticide used to kill varroa. 
**Checkstan**: Varroa treatment and small hive beetle treatment. 
**Mite Away Quick Strip**: (MAQS)
	- 7 day single application 
	- against varroa and tracheal mites.
