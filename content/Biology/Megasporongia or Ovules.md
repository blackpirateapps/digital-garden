1. Ovule is a small structure attacted to the placenta. It is a part of the [[Gynocium]] of the [[Flower]].
2. It is ==attached to the placenta by a stalk called funicle==. 
3. The place where the body of the ovule fuses with funicle is called **Hilum**. 
4. Hilum represents the junction between ovule and funicle. 
5. Each ovule is covered by one or two protective envelope called ==**Integuments**==.
6. Enclosed within **Integuments** is a mass of cell called **neucellus**. Cells of neucellus has abundunt food material. 
7. Integuments cover the whole neucellus except at the tip where a small opening is formed, which is called **micropyle**. 
8. **Opposite of the micropyler end** is **Chalaza**. Which is the basal part of the ovule. 
9. **Embryo sac or female gametophyte** is located inside neucellus. 
10. One ovule generally has one embryo sac. 

Megaspore mother cell takes part in [[Megasporogenesis]]. 

### Female gametophyte / Embryo Sac
1. In a majority of flowering plants **Only one of the megaspore is functional** and **other three degenerate.** 
2. Only the functional gametophyte (embryo sac) devlops into embryo sac or female gametophyte. 
3. Formation of embryo sac from one megaspore is called ==Monosporic devlopment==. 

#### Formation of Embryo Sac
1. The neucellus of the fuctional megaspore divides mitotically to form two neuclei which moves to the opposite poles and forms **2 neuclete** stage. 
2. Two more mitotic division happens, which makes **4 neuclete** and **8 neuclete** stage respectively. 
3. All these divisions are only neucler, that means **neuclear divisions are not followed immediately by cell wall formation.**
4. After 8 neuclete stage cell walls are formed. 
5. **6 out of 8 neucles are srrounded by cell walls** remaining two neuclei called **polar neuclei** are situated below egg apparatus in the large central cell.
6. **The egg apparatus** consists of **three cells at the micropylar end**. 
7. of these three cells **One is egg cell and other two are synergids**. 
8. Synergids have special cellular thickenings at the micropylar end called **filiform apparatus**.It helps in guiding the pollen tube into the synergid.
9. The three cells at the chalazal end are called **antipodals**. 
10. The large central cell has two polar neuclei. 
11. Typical angiosperm embryo sac is **8 neuclete and 7 celled**.
