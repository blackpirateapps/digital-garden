Ovary is the basal buldged part of the pistil in [[Gynocium]] in [[Flower]]. 
- The cavity of the ovary is called **Ovarian Cavity or ==Locule==**. 
- Inside Locule **Placenta** is located. 
- Arising form placenta there is **[[Megasporongia or Ovules]].**
- The number of [[Megasporongia or Ovules]] may be one (wheat, paddy, mango) or many (watermelon, papaya, orchids).
