A flower is a ==modified shoot== with highly condensed internodes and leaves are specialised variously to act as different floral parts of organs. 

## Structures of a flower
A flower has 
1. [[Adroecium]]
2. [[Gynocium]]
3. [[Calyx]]
4. [[Corolla]]

> Cultivation of ornamental plants for their flowers is called **Floriculture**
