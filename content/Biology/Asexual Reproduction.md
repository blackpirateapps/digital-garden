## Characteristics of asexual reproduction
---
1. No gametes would be formed. 
2. Most of the time it does not need two individual organism. 
3. fertilisation does not happen. 
4. New offsprings are morphologically and genetically identical so they are called **clones**. 
5. Asexual reproductions are common among single-celled organisms, and it plants or animals with relatively simple organisations. 
6. In protists and monerans the organism or the parent cell divides into two to give rise to new individuals, so in this case cell division is a form of reproduction. 


## Types of Asexual reproduction
---
### Fission
1. It is the division of parent cell to give rise to two or more daughter individuals identical to the parent. It is of three types: 
	1. **Binary Fission** -> Depending on the plane of division it is of different types, 
		1. **Simple -> Amoeba**
		2. **Longitudinal -> Euglina**
		3. **Transverse -> *Paramecium***
	2. **Multiple Fission** -> *Amoeba, Plasmodium*
	3. **Plasmotomy** -> *Opalina*

### Budding
Daughter indivisual is formed form a small part or bud. The bud gets seperated and forms new individual. 
**E.g -> yeast, *hydra***

### Gemmae 
These are unicellular or multicellular propagules which devlop in small receptacles called **gemma cups**. They detach form the parent body and grow into individuals. 
**E.g -> *Marchantia***

### Sporulation
Under unfavourable condition amoeba withdraws it's **Pseudopodia** and secrete a three layered hard covering around itself. This process is called **encystation** . When the favourable condition returns amoeba divides itself by multiple fission and gives rise to multiple amoeba or pseudopo to spores.  The cyst walls brusts and thse spores gets librated to the medium to form amoeba. This Phenomenon is called **Sporulation**. 

### Regeneration**E.g -> 
The capacity to regenrate new individual from a fragmented part is called **Regeneration**. 
It is of two types. 
1. Morphallaxis -> Whole body is formed from a small fragment. **E.g -> Hydra**
2. Epimorphosis -> Replacement of lost body part. **e.g -> broken tail of wall lizard**

### Fragmentation
When parent body breaks into two or more fragments and each fragment gives rise to a new individual. **e.g -> *Spirogyra*, sponges, echinoderms**. 

### Other asexual ways to reproduce
1. Members of kingdom fungi and simple plants like alge reproduce through special structures called spores. 
2. Common asexual reproductive structures are -
	1. **Zoospores -> motile and flagellated, e.g -> *Chlamydomonas***
	2. **Conidia -> non motile and produced exogenously -> e.g -> *Penicillium*
	3. **Chlanydospores -> thick walled non motile -> e.g -> *Rizopus***
	4. **Oidia -> small, thin-walled fragments -> *Agaricus***
	5. **Sporangiospores -> non-motile endospores -> *Mucor***
	6. **Gemmules -> endogenous bud -> sponges**

## Vegetative Reproduction
1. The unit of vegetative reproduction are called vegetative propagules. 
2. Examples of few vegetative propagules -
	1. **Runner -> *Oxalis***
	2. **rhizome -> ginger**
	3. **sucker -> banana**
	4. **tuber -> potato**
	5. **offset -> *Eichhornia***
	6. **bulb ->. garlic**
3. **Water hyacinth also called 'terror of bengal' is one of the most invasive weed introduced in India for its beautiful flowers and leaves.**
4. There are many artificial ways of vegetative reproduction, this is done to get desired characters. These methods are -> *cutting* (**steam cutting in sugercane, rose, leaf cutting in *Sansevieria*  root cutting in blackberry**), *layering* ( soil layering in jasmine and air layering in litchi ), *grafting* ( mango, apple ).
