1. Pollen grains are the first cell of a male gametophyte. 

### Structure
1. It is 25-50 micrometeres in diameter. 
2. Pollen grain's protoplast is **unineucleate in the beginning but at the time of libretion it becomes 2-3 celled.**
3. Covering of the pollen grain is called ==sporoderm==
4. It has two layers. 
	1. Exine -> Made up of **sporopollenin**, provides protection. There is certain areas where exine remains thin. These places are called **germ pores**
	> Sporopollenin is one of the most resistant organic material known. It can withstand high temparature, strong acids and alkali. No enzymes can degrade sporopollenin, so far known. Because of this pollen grains are preserved for long periods in fossils. 
	2. Intine -> It is the layer under extine. 
5. The size of the necleus increseas and it divides **mitotically** to produce a bigger **Vegetative cell and a smaller generative cell**
6. Vegetative cell is big, has abundunt food reserve and has large irregular shaped neucleus. 
7. Generative cell is small, spindle shaped, dense cytoplasm and a neucleus. 
8. ==In over 60 percent of angiosperms pollen grains are shed at this stage==(two celled stage). In remaining species the generative cell divides mitotically to give rise to two male gametes before pollen grains are shed. (3- celled stage)
9. Pollen grains of many species can cause severe allergies and bronchial affilictions in some people often leading to chronic respiratory diseases like **asthma, bronchitis** etc.
10. Pollen grains are rich in neutrients, it is possible to use pollen grains as food suppliments. ==Pollen consumption has been claimed to increase the performance of atheletes and race horses==
11. Pollen grains remain viable for a variable amount of time. 
12. In cereals like **rice and wheat** pollen grains lose viability within **30 mins** of their release.  In some members of **Rosaceae, Leguminoseae and Solanaceae,** they maintain viability for months. 
13. Pollen grains can be preserved in liquid nitrogen (-196 degree C).
